## Chocolate Brownie Cake

## Ingredients

* 420g Sugar
* 165g AP Flour
* 50g Cocoa Powder
* 50g powdered sugar
* 1 gram salt
* 3 Large Eggs
* 220g butter
* 180g Semi-sweet Chocolate
* 1 tbsp vanilla paste/extract
* 15g room temperature water

## Method

1. Melt chocolate and butter over a double boiler (bain-marie - bowl over a pot of simmering water)
2. Whisk the eggs in another bowl and slowly add the chocolate-butter mix, ensuring it's not too hot or too sudden or it will scramble the eggs
3. Add the water and vanilla paste/extract to the mix above
4. In a separate bowl, combine all the sugar, flour, cocoa powder and salt
5. Combine the chocolate-butter mix with the dry ingredients and fold until smooth and no clumps remain
6. Pour mixture into a butter-greased, parchment paper-lined cake tin and bake at 165°C for 40-45 minutes
